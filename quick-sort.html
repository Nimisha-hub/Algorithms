How it works

1. Choose a pivot: The last element of the current subarray is selected.

2. Partition: Elements smaller than the pivot move to its left; larger elements move to its right.

3. Place pivot: The pivot reaches its final sorted position.

4. Recurse: Quick Sort repeats the process on the left and right subarrays.

Pseudocode

QUICKSORT(A, low, high) if low < high: p = PARTITION(A, low, high) QUICKSORT(A, low, p-1) QUICKSORT(A, p+1, high)
