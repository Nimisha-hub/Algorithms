How it works

1. Look in the middle: pick the middle penguin of the range between lo and hi.

2. Compare: if it matches, you're done. If it's too small, the target must be to the right.

3. Drop half: move lo or hi past mid, so half of the penguins are ruled out in one go.

4. Repeat until you find it, or lo passes hi, meaning it isn't there.

The list must be sorted. Each step halves the range, so it takes about log₂(n) steps.
